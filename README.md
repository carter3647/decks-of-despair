# Decks of Despair
Decks of Despair (full name is 'Decks of Doom, Despair and Everything Unfair') is a card game about attacking, blocking damage, tanking damage, playing support, and using strategy to win this chaotic game.
Spades are attacks,
Diamonds are block,
Hearts are health,
and Clubs are support cards.
## How to play
The game moves counter-clockwise.
Pick a player to go first, and then let them play their turn
When it is your turn, pick up 2 cards from the draw pile.
Make sure that your support is discarded and at the end of your turn, you're under 7 cards.
When the drawpile is empty, use the discard pile as the new drawpile.(Remember to reverse the order!)
In a turn, you can do 3 things.
1) You can play an attack.
   
   If you attack somebody that doesn't have enough diamond cards that add up equal to or higher than the damage of your spade,
   they will have to use hearts instead.
   
   They can choose whether to use hearts or block,
   but the main difference is that hearts get put in the Banish pile,
   while block gets put in Discard.

   Hearts and block cannot be added up together,
   and if you can't either block an attack or tank with hearts, you're out!

   You can also use the Ace of Clubs as your attack,
   which lets you see the person you picked's cards,
   and steal anything that isn't an Ace or a Joker.

   If you have an Ace of Spades, you can play that as an attack,
   and then your opponent will be forced to use hearts instead!
   It will deal damage equal to a king. (13)

2) You can play a support card.

   Support is played in 4 forms.
   Low-tier support,
   High-tier support,
   Face-card support,
   and block.

   Support is played face-down, and is revealed when the person after you attacks or skips their turn.
   Once you play support, the next time it its your turn, it will trigger.

   A low-tier support is any club below 7.(Not including Aces.)
   When triggered, it will let you draw an extra card from the draw pile. It is then discarded.
   
   A high-tier support is any club above 7, but not including face cards.
   When triggered, it will let you draw a card from the top of the Discard pile. The card itself is then discarded.
   
   A face-card support is, well, a face card, and when it activates, you have to either play it as a lower support,
   or pick a player to steal from. When stealing with a face card, the person being stolen from has to pick a card to give you.
   Just like Ace of Clubs, you cannot steal Aces or Jokers.

   Using block as support will allow you to pair that with another block card, but will be
   discarded upon activation.
